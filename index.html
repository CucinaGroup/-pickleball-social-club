Clarice's Pickleball Social Club ✨

How it works
  1. Check in when you arrive. Only players who are here get drawn.
  2. Partners are picked at random. Any extra players wait on standby.
  3. Each game is played to 11 points.
  4. Win and your team moves on to the next round. Lose and you're out.
  5. When your match is called, go to the court straight away. If you're not there, you're out and your partner plays with a random standby player.
  6. If both partners are missing, or no one is on standby, it's a default defeat.
  7. The last team standing is the champion.
