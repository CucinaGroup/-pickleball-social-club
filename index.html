Clarice's Pickleball Social Club ✨

How it works
  1. Check in when you arrive. Only players who are here get drawn. Extra players wait on standby.
  2. Every round, partners are picked at random again.
  3. Each game is played to 11 points.
  4. Win and you move on to the next round with a new random partner. Lose and you're out.
  5. If the players left don't split evenly into games of 4, 2 players get a bye and move on.
  6. When your match is called, go to the court straight away. If you're not there, you're out and your partner plays with a random standby player.
  7. If both partners are missing, or no one is on standby, it's a default defeat.
  8. When 2 players are left, they play a singles final. The winner is the champion.
