Clarice's Pickleball Social Club ✨

How it works
  1. Check in when you arrive. Only players who are here get drawn. Extra players wait on standby.
  2. Every team is 1 girl and 1 boy. Partners are picked at random again every round.
  3. Each game is played to 11 points.
  4. Win and you move on with a new random partner. Lose once and you're out.
  5. If the teams left don't split evenly into games, some players sit out that round.
  6. When your match is called, go to the court straight away. If you're not there, you're out and your partner plays with a random standby player (a girl replaces a girl, a boy replaces a boy).
  7. If both partners are missing, or no suitable standby player is free, it's a default defeat.
  8. When 4 players are left, they play the final. The winning girl and boy are the champions.
